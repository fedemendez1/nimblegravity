Hi Federico
Hope you're well.
Good news! The team has completed the review of your case study and we'd like to move things to the next stage, which is the technical interview.

During this session you'll meet with: Bart de Groot (Analytics Lead) and Ekaterina Bogdanova (Lead Analyst)
Please use this link to pick the day and time that works best for you - https://app-de.cronofy.com/rts/A16HZI_Q. If none of the available slots work for you, please do let us know when would be best and I'll see what the team can do.
The interview will last 60 minutes and will consist of 4 parts:

* (10 min) Introductions and interview format
* (15 - 20min) Case Study Presentation + Q&A - be prepared to present your submission and answer any questions around your approach, findings and recommendations.
* Problem Solving/Scenario - Scenario based questions to assess your analytical thinking and problem solving approach in a hypothetical scenario.
* (5-10 min) Q&A - Your opportunity to ask the team questions about Wise, the team and the role. Be sure to come prepared with some questions (ideally 5+) that will help making decisions about the role later on in the process.

What We're Looking For:

* Problem-solving approach and logical thinking
* Ability to work through challenges step-by-step
* Clear communication of your thought process

The main skills we are assessing:

* Problem solving: Your recommendations, customer focus and ability to provide strong reasoning for chosen methodologies when challenged
* Presentation & Communication: Structure and flow of the presentation, visualisations, presenting in a clear and concise manner

I've put together some useful hints/tips to help you prepare...

Prepare examples of:

- A successful project you have delivered.
- A project that did not achieve the desired outcome.

Be ready to discuss:

- The business problem.
- Your specific contribution.
- How success was measured.
- Customer and business impact.
- Key learnings and what you would do differently.

## Prioritisation and Decision Making

Be prepared to explain how you manage competing priorities.

Consider examples that demonstrate:

- Balancing multiple requests and stakeholder needs.
- Using business impact, customer value, and data to prioritise work.
- Making decisions when resources or time are limited.

## Marketing Analytics Knowledge

Refresh your understanding of:

- Key marketing channels and their objectives.
- Customer acquisition, engagement, and retention metrics.
- Marketing measurement and performance analysis.
- Referral, invite, or word-of-mouth growth programmes (if applicable).

Focus on how data and insights can be used to improve marketing performance
and customer outcomes.

## Problem Solving and Ambiguity

Prepare examples where:

- The problem was not clearly defined.
- Requirements evolved over time.
- You had to translate a business question into an analytical approach.
- You worked with incomplete information to drive a recommendation.

Interviewers will be looking for structured thinking and a practical
approach to solving open-ended problems.

## Commercial Acumen

Be ready to discuss:

- How your analysis influenced business decisions.
- Trade-offs between growth, efficiency, customer experience, and
  profitability.
- How you measure and communicate business impact.

## Stakeholder Management and Influence

Prepare examples that demonstrate your ability to:

- Partner effectively with marketing stakeholders.
- Translate technical findings into clear business recommendations.
- Influence decision-making using data and insights.
- Build strong relationships across teams.

## Preparation Tips

- Use a structured format (e.g. STAR: Situation, Action, Result) for
  project examples.
- Quantify impact wherever possible.
- Clearly explain your individual contribution.
- Focus on outcomes, business value, and customer impact, not just the
  analysis itself.
- Be prepared to explain your thinking and approach, not just the final
  result.
