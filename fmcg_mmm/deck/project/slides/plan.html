Five moves to keep growing revenue

Price

Hold the gap to competitors; test any further increase with one retailer first

Promotions

Cut promo depth and frequency; trial a promo-light period with one retailer

Distribution

Protect the 2024 listings; target stores where competitors are stocked and we aren't

Summer

Agree stock and display plans with retailers that switch on with heat forecasts

Media

Hold budget, weight it to spring–summer, and stagger channels so each one can be measured

The plan follows the evidence: price and distribution are the big levers; promotions and media are small and should earn their keep. Each move is a step we can test and read in the next model refresh. Promo savings can fund distribution and summer activation.
