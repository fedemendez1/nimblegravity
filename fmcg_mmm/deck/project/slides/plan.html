Revenue a year vs 2024, before margin. Source: marketing mix model.

No single total on purpose. Discounts and media are sized moves: £6.5m to £11m of revenue a year versus 2024. Distribution is shown per point because the gain depends on what we hold: each point of weighted distribution is worth ~£6m a year (if we hold today's ~46% all year versus the 2024 average of 43.5%, about £14m). Confidence differs: discounts is the most solid (model plus accounting); distribution is the largest lever, but treat £6m a point as an upper estimate since retailers also list what sells; media is an early read with a wide range and its real value is making each channel measurable. Next steps: geo lift for media (hold out one or two ITV regions per pulse), a price test with one retailer before any further rise (we are at the top of the price gaps we have seen), and a model refresh in 6 to 12 months with retailer-level data. Revenue, not profit.
