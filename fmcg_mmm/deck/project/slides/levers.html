Distribution is the biggest lever. Discounts are the money to free up.

Revenue at 2024 levels, short term, before margin. Source: marketing mix model.

One currency for every lever, all from the same model. Distribution: +1 point of weighted distribution adds ~3.6% volume, about £6m of revenue a year at 2024 levels (range £5.3m to £6.7m). Media: £1.06 of revenue per £1 (range £0.65 to £1.66). Price: elasticity −0.47 vs competitors, so +1% relative price loses ~0.5% volume but adds ~0.5% revenue, about £0.8m; valid within the price gaps we have seen (we are now near the top). Discounts: £0.13 of incremental revenue per £1 of discount (range nothing to £0.42). Weather is the other big driver (+4% volume per degree above 20°C in a week) but it is not a lever. Revenue, not profit: we have no margins.
