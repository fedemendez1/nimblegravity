Promotions: promoted volume share doubled to a third, yet the model finds no measurable uplift from promo depth or breadth: most promoted volume would have sold anyway. Media: ~£1 of revenue per £1, short term, before margin. Same answer with and without model assumptions (unconstrained check: £1.26). Channels that ran together can only be read together. The 2024 spring-summer launch did better than the 2023 early-spring burst; one plausible reason is timing with demand, which we propose to test. Media is a small lever for this brand: under 1% of volume.
