Discount = volume × (shelf price − price paid), before any retailer funding. Source: client data, marketing mix model.

Discount given away = volume x (shelf price - price actually paid): £2.8m in 2022, £10.1m in 2023, £14.9m in 2024 (8% of revenue at shelf price). Promoted share of volume went from 16% to 33%. The model finds almost no extra volume from promotions: about +1.2% of 2024 volume, worth ~£2m, so ~£0.13 back per £1 of discount (likely range: nothing to £0.42). Scenario, back to 2022 promotion levels on 2024 volumes: discounts fall by ~£11m, volume revenue lost ~£1.5m (at worst ~£4.8m), net +£6m to +£10m a year. Caveats: we do not know who funds the discounts (brand vs retailer) or the trade terms; promotions may be part of what secured the 2024 listings, so cut gradually and watch distribution; long-term brand effects are not measured; revenue, not profit.
