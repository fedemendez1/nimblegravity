Volume due-to: price cost us, distribution won it back

Points of change vs the previous year's volume. All other: temperature, rain, calendar, competitor media, unexplained. Source: MMM.

How to read a due-to: volume changed by X% versus last year; each bar is how many of those percentage points are due to each driver, and they add up to the total. 2023 vs 2022, volume −8.2%: relative price took 7.7 points, Brand C promotions 1.8, cooler summer weeks 0.7; our promotions and media added about half a point each. 2024 vs 2023, volume +8.2%: distribution added 13.3 points, Brand C easing its promotions 1.7; relative price took 4.3 and cooler summers 1.6; media and our promotions together under 1 point. Base drivers move volume by whole points; incremental activity (promotions, media) by fractions of a point. Every bar comes from the marketing mix model; media channels are detailed in the appendix.
