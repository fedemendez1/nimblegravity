Read left to right. "Relative price" = our shelf price vs competitors', so it moves with both. 2023: the price increase cost ~13m kg (−8%); nothing else moved much. 2024: distribution gains added ~20m kg (+13%), more than offsetting a further price effect. Media and promotions add under 2m kg in either year. If asked: these effects are measured holding everything else equal; "Other" in % of volume, 2023 / 2024: weather -1.4 / -1.6 (each summer since 2022 a little cooler), competitors -1.8 / +1.5 (almost all Brand C promotions: share of its volume on promo went 3% to 6% to 4%; competitor media has no measurable effect; competitor prices sit in the Relative price bar), calendar and unexplained +1.8 / -1.7. Price: our shelf price +31% then +7%, competitors +10% then -2%, so the price gap widened most in 2023 (that is why the 2023 price hit is twice 2024's).
