Effect of a 10% higher price premium vs competitors, all else equal. Source: marketing mix model.

Price elasticity vs competitors is about −0.45: a 10% higher premium loses ~4.5% of volume but adds ~5% revenue. Robust across every model version we tested (−0.41 to −0.50). Caution: the premium went from 1.5x to 1.9x; the further it goes, the less we can say from history. Revenue, not profit: we have no margin data.
