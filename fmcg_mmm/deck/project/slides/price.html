Marketing mix model what-if, all else equal. Likely range: volume −3.6% to −5.3%, revenue +4% to +6%.

Price elasticity vs competitors is −0.47: a 10% higher premium loses ~4.5% of volume but adds ~5% revenue. Robust across every model version we tested (−0.41 to −0.50). The +10% is a what-if, not a plan: it applies the measured elasticity (−0.47, 90% range −0.56 to −0.38) to a 10% higher price vs competitors; revenue = 1.10 x 0.955 = +5%. Caution: the premium went from 1.5x to 1.95x; the further it goes, the less we can say from history. Revenue, not profit: we have no margin data.
