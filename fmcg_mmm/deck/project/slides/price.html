Elasticity −0.47 (90% range −0.56 to −0.38), stable across every model version we tested (−0.41 to −0.50). Inelastic: a 1% higher price vs competitors loses about 0.5% of volume, so revenue still rises. Important: this is the elasticity over the prices we have observed (1.3x to 2.1x competitors). Elasticity usually rises as the gap widens, and bottled water is easy to substitute (other brands, private label, tap). We are at the top of the observed range, so the history cannot tell us how shoppers would react to more. Hence: hold the gap and learn with a controlled test (one retailer or region, a few months) before any further increase. Revenue, not profit: we have no margin data.
