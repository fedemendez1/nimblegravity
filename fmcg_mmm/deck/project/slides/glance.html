Set the scene before the moves. Volume: 167m kg in 2022, 154m in 2023, 166m in 2024 (2022 had 53 weeks), so flat over three years. Price: our shelf price per kg rose about 40% (£0.77 to £1.08) while competitors rose about 8%, so we went from 1.5 times their price to 1.95 times. Price sensitivity (if asked): elasticity about −0.47 versus competitors, so +1% relative price costs about 0.5% volume but adds about 0.5% revenue; measured within the price gaps we have seen, and we are now at the top of that range. Distribution (share of stores stocking us, weighted by store size) averaged 39% in 2022 and 44% in 2024, and is about 46% today. Weather is the biggest outside factor: each degree above 20°C in a week adds about 4% volume, and 2022 had a record heatwave. Media: about £3.5m over three years (£0.2m in 2022, £1.2m in 2023, £2.1m in 2024) and well under 1% of volume; it pays back about £1 in sales per £1. Promotions also grew a lot (16% to 33% of volume on promotion), covered on the discounts slide. The next slide shows how these pieces add up to the change in volume each year.
