Distribution = share of stores stocking us, weighted by store size. Source: marketing mix model.

Distribution is the single biggest volume driver: +1 point of weighted distribution is worth ~3.6% volume (90% range 3.2–4.0%). Weather: each degree of weekly maximum above 20°C adds ~4% (3.7–4.6%). We cannot control weather, but we can control availability and visibility in those weeks.
