Track distribution weekly as the #1 KPI: every point lost costs ~£6m a year.

Distribution = share of stores stocking us, weighted by store size. Revenue at 2024 levels. Source: marketing mix model.

Each point of weighted distribution adds ~3.6% volume (range 3.2–4.0%), about £6m of revenue a year. Distribution fell from ~41% in H1 2022 to ~37% in H2 2022; the model puts the cost at ~12m kg, about £10m, in six months. It then recovered to 46% by summer 2024 and is ~46% in January 2025. Holding 46% through 2025 versus the 2024 average of 43.5% is worth ~2.4 points, about £14m of revenue. How: a weekly distribution dashboard by retailer with alerts on any drop; prioritise listings defence in trade negotiations; a point is worth paying for if it costs less than £6m a year, and part of the discount savings can fund it. If asked about cause and effect: weighted distribution reflects listings, not weekly sales, so it does not move with weather (correlation 0.00; stores-selling did, 0.43, and was dropped for that reason). Retailers do list what sells, so treat £6m a point as an upper estimate; the H2-2022 dip shows the cost of losing it. This is all-commodity distribution, not category-weighted.
