Distribution = share of stores stocking us, weighted by store size. Source: client data, marketing mix model.

Distribution is the single biggest volume driver: +1 point of weighted distribution is worth ~3.6% volume (90% range 3.2–4.0%). It dipped to ~37% in the second half of 2022, sat near 40% in 2023 and rose to ~46% in summer 2024; that gain added about 20m kg (+13%) in 2024, more than offsetting the price effect. Caveat if asked: this is all-commodity distribution (stores weighted by total sales), not category-weighted; retailers also list what sells, so part of the link may run both ways.
