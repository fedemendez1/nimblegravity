Media returns about £1.06 of revenue per £1 (likely range £0.65 to £1.66), short term and before margin. Same answer without model assumptions (unconstrained check: £1.26). Campaigns, likely ranges: 2024 video + outdoor £1.10 (£0.49 to £2.19), social £0.98 (£0.32 to £2.60), 2023 TV + partnership £0.86 (£0.42 to £1.58). Channels launched together cannot be separated, so we read them as campaigns. The 2024 spring-summer launch did better than the 2023 early-spring burst; one plausible reason is timing with demand, which we propose to test. Media is a small lever for this brand: under 1% of volume.
