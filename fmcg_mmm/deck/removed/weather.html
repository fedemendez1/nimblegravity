Agree stock and display plans with retailers that switch on when a heatwave is forecast.

Weather is the second big driver after distribution, and it is not in our control, but readiness is. Each degree of weekly peak temperature above 20°C adds ~4% volume (90% range 3.7–4.6%), on top of the normal seasonal pattern. About 19 weeks a year cross 20°C. The hottest week in our data (July 2022, peak ~34°C) sold 4.9m kg, 44% above a normal summer week (3.4m kg). Losing those weeks to empty shelves is the biggest avoidable loss. Recent summers were slightly cooler, which cost ~1.5% volume each year. On-slide footnote (removed): Peak = highest daily temperature of the week. Normal summer week = June–August, peak under 23°C. Source: marketing mix model.
